  Guest review with Efstathios, he was needed for rating as this was my second
  shawarma of the day. Very clean place and very friendly workers. The price was
  unreal, $11.29 for a shawarma that almost competed with flaming stove for
  size. Almost… complimentary tea won us over for experience. Tag on great AC,
  great washrooms, hard to find a reason to not give it 25/25 for experience,
  but we decided not to because of location and the hope that a 25 exists
  somewhere else. We have to talk about the turnips. They didn’t exist. I’m
  cutting them some slack because it’s Turkish-style. Regardless vegetables were
  lackluster to say the damn least. We also have to talk about how oily/greasy
  it was. First bites had me thinking this spot was going to give flaming stove
  a run for its money. Lackluster vegetables and oily, not spicy shawarma took
  away from the great meat and amazing bread this place has going. What could’ve
  been Lezzet12! Less is more except when it comes to turnips! More is more.
