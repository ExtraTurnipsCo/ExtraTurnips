note: >-
  Another pita-style shawarma here with that characteristic warm cloud-like
  fluffy bread. They managed to stuff this thing really well for the size of the
  pita to be fair. The tzatziki they snuck in there was quite nice, garlic sauce
  as well and an interesting green hot sauce made for a really enjoyable sauce
  setup. Chicken was really juicy but had some crisp, lots of really small
  shaved pieces which I enjoyed. No turnips or pickles but lettuce onion tomato
  trifecta was fresh, all be it not my first choice. 10.75 pounds and it wasnt
  huge but I will say we were right in central where everythings pricey. Sylvia
  the worker was super cool to. Only outdoor store pretty much.
