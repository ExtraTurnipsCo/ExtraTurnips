note: >-
  Hip little store right in the heart of London just south of Hyde Park,
  slinging out lots of different sandwiches and wraps with a chicken shawarma
  being one of them. I think they’re an offshoot of a popular Lebanese
  restaurant and the shawarma was tasty. Sauce was the standout, pickles a close
  second. Chicken was a bit of a weak link, they were just grilling… no spit in
  sight which may have explained it. Regardless it was a good, reasonably sized
  unit for £7.75. Experience was quick, place is small but cool, trendy even.
  Would love more seating though could see it getting problematic if it was
  busy. Overall cool spot in a busy area.
