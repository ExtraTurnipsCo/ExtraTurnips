note: >-
  This is a place I passed many times but never went into, partially because
  it's in such a competitive neighborhood for food in the Annex. Regardless, got
  the chance and it was sturdy. Good sauces, solid veggies, and great service.
  They also had a patio that was lovely looking over Bloor St W. 


  The chicken was dry though, almost like a chicken breast rather than the
  greasy, melt in your mouth thigh we're used to. As well, the bread got soggy
  pretty quickly which hurt the overall integrity of the wrap, and once you lose
  the integrity it's hard to comeback from that. This place is still worth a
  visit though if you're in the area, I would consider going back if it's
  convenient.
