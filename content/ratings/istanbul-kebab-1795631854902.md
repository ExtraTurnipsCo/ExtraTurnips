note: >-
  Ended up out Hackney way to work a shift behind the bar and stopped in at this
  fine local establishment beforehand. Felt a little bourdainy eating at the
  standing tables with my winter coat on at this shop. Kinda gave me some upper
  east side, quick bite, all metal table type of vibes. Anyway, the wrap was
  solid, it was a chicken doner technically, and it came with a drink for 7
  pounds. It was pretty hefty so the value was there although we were a bit off
  the main drag. Sauces I find lead the way with the doner kebabs, its a
  creamier tzatziki esque sauce usually then a chunkier chili sauce for the
  spice and they're tasty and powerful, definitely a highlight. Veggies were
  also nice with red cabbage making an appearance along with the usual suspects,
  veggies seemed fresh. Chicken was nothing to write home about though, the
  sauce helped it out .
