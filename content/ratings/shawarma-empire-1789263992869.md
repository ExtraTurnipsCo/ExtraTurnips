  Came here with my dad after a round of hungover golf and I was pleasantly

  rice, sharp tangy turnips (got extra), chicken was great to. Came with a side
  of warm pita as well. I've had better sauces, and I did feel like by the end
  it was down to just rice but that could've been my fault. Overall solid
  experience, it was pretty busy but we ate inside and it felt authentic, food
  kinda took a while though and the process wasn't crystal clear still a super
  solid spot.
