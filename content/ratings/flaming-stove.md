note: >-
  This place is elite. Knocks it out of the park on taste, you will struggle to
  find better in the GTA. The Saj style wraps are expertly crafted with an
  amazing cross-section and the wrap holds up until the last bite. The only
  knock we had was the toppings and the sauces. The toppings were good not
  great. Sauces were confusing and too mayo-ey for our liking, despite the wrap
  being dry at times. Our guest reviewer Eden loved the turnips and said this
  spot has the best shawarma she has ever tasted (She is inexpereinced). Finally
  on value, we are two big hungry guys and each had our own; however, this wrap
  could easily be split into two meals, which is why value was high despite an
  above average cost for the area.
