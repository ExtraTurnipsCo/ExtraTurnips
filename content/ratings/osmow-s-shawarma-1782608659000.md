  Osmow's is a pretty popular shawarma spot. It's the biggest shawarma chain in

  shawarma that fills you up, quick service with good seating, and good
  condiments and sauces. It's easy to see how it got so big, Osmow's has
  perfected mediocre shawarma. There's nothing I found special about this
  shawarma, but nothing I disliked. The score reflects this in all three
  categories, and yields a score higher than I would've expected. A caveat here
  was this order took place after Austin and I had some drinks. We tried to stay
  professional and we are happy to do a re-review if the community calls this
  spot into question.
