note: >-
  This place is special. My first question after my experience is what’s the
  catch but as I get further away from the establishment on my way home I
  realize there may not be one. Table service after you order and it’s 6.99, and
  I got a wrap, fries, little pickle/turnip salad, 2 good sauces (a tangy hot
  sauce and a garlic) and a drink. Come on. Wrap wasn’t huge but it was a decent
  size. Only obvious knock I can make right now is the structural integrity of
  the wrap. The bread was good but soft and it wasn’t wrapped tight which led to
  spillage. I had a plate and utensils so crisis was averted, but a wrap is
  meant to be eaten with your hands like we gotta think about the to-go people
  here. Other than that I can’t come up with another real non nitpicking issue.
  I’m glad they tossed plenty of turnips on the platter, chicken was really nice
  and had that nice crisped exterior. I will say though when you put fries on a
  plate you now stand to be evaluated on the grand scheme of fries which isn’t
  my specialty but I understand to be a dangerous game in its own right. Overall
  they’re fucking killing it in there they let people smoke on the patio and
  there’s people ripping shishas to it’s kind of awesome, gave me the vibe that
  it would be lowkey busy on a Friday night.
