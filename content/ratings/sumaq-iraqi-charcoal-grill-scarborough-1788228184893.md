note: >-
  It was never our intention to go here, but when we passed it on route to our
  original destination we knew what we had to do. The place was slammed busy at
  9pm on a Monday, and they had an assembly line rivaling a King St Chipotle at
  noon. It was roughly 14 bucks for a solid size shawarma, skinny and long. 


  Taste was the x factor here, such good bread, garlicky sauces, and chicken in
  abundance. I do wish they loaded up more turnips onto the wrap though cause
  man they were good, the plates seemed like they had more. Overall they have an
  absolute operation going there and if you're remotely close by, or have
  nothing better to do, its worth a visit.
