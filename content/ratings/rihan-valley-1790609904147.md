  Sneaky special performance out of Rihan Valley today. An unassuming shop
  operating well within its means as a one man show, this place got it done in
  all important categories.


  Experience, guy was great. I asked for extra turnips, we chatted about it and
  he explained how they’re better on the falafel. Place was clean, outdoor
  seating, ashtray if you need it. 

  Value, really solid. 7 pounds seems like a solid number kind of 13.50 Canadian
  maybe plays a bit lower even given I’m in London… but the size shocked me. It
  had length and girth to it and could be considered a meal in itself.

  Finally taste again just got it done everywhere. If you’re not a big garlicky
  super saucy shawarma fan you’d like this one it doesn’t have creamy sauces
  spilling out just good veggies, moist chicken, and some hot sauce and their
  chill garlic sauce. Bread was nice and crisped to which I enjoyed… I predict
  I’ll be back
