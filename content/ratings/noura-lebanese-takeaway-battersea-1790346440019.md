note: >-
  Here we are in London, first place I tried purely out of being nearby when I
  got hungry and it was solid. Starting with taste, which was the best quality,
  the sauce was awesome. Garlicky and plentiful. Pickles were tangy which I
  loved, and the chicken had solid flavour but it was on the drier side for my
  liking. Bread kind of felt just like a pita, but structurally the wrap did a
  decent job. We aren't in the business of rating french fries but it came with
  some and they were decent to.


  Value's tough cause I'm not in Canada. 10 pounds for a drink wrap and some
  fries sounds great but that gets you to like 18.75 cad which doesn't stack up
  great. May need to re-work the value situation but for now it is what it is.


  Experience was solid, patio was great and I chatted with the guy for a sec
  when he heard my american accent. There was no inside area really which was a
  little confusing but overall decent experience. He started smoking basically
  right beside me and I can't decide if it made my experience better or worse.
