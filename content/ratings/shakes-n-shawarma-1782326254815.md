note: >-
  This wasn't a planned stop but as I passed it with Eden while walking around
  the Annex my stomach decided it was time. The place was pretty empty, it was
  11:15 am after all. For me the highlight was the sauces, they were flavorful
  and the right amount of hot sauce was on there for sure.


  Other than that though, it didn't blow me away anywhere else. Doesn't mean it
  was bad, for $11 I would even consider going back if in a pinch. But the
  chicken wasn't super flavorful, there was to much lettuce for my liking, and
  worst of all it was falling apart at the seams only a few bites in. This may
  have been a fluke but had I been eating in the store I would've made an
  absolute mess. Luckily I was outside and the Spadina Ave pigeons were able to
  clean up what I couldn't.
