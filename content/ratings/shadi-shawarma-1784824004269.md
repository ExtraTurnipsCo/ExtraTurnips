note: >-
  Re-visited a place in the West End we’ve been going to for a long time after
  golf rounds and it did not disappoint. For starters, the main guy remembered
  me… that’s an awesome feeling and just shows how much they care especially
  when you keep coming back.


  I got the student special as usual which is a shawarma wrap and a side of
  potatoes with sauce for $10 bucks. The garlic sauce was great could’ve been a
  little more garlicky but that might be a preference thing for me. Shawarma was
  loaded with good chicken, not life-changing chicken but good solid seasoned
  protein carrying chicken. After that it did feel like lettuce was acting as a
  bit of a filler. Nailed the hot sauce levels though, and they had a nice
  variety of sauces they put on and didn’t overdo. 


  The experience was great, they were friendly, there’s seating indoor & outdoor
  although outdoors minimal but it’s a great little table. No bathrooms though
  which actually would’ve been nice in the moment. 

  Shadi Shawarma is a great cheap spot in the west end that more often than not
  when I pass it I get it.
