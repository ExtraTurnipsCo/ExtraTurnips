Mcdonalds has its place in society. Cheap food that isn't the best for you and isn't the tastiest has real value. Lezzet12 is the Mcdonalds of Shawarma.
I gave a 35.5/50 on taste to Lezzet12. Why this place sounds like a gamertag beats me. I thought of the reasons why. A sanity check was me taking 5 off for no turnips, 5 off for lackluster vegetables, 5 off for sauces that caused the wrap to be oily. That would make it 35/50, it had great accompaniments of bread, amazing cross-section.
