preamble: Whenever we had days off, we'd find ourselves in some random part of
  Toronto at a Shawarma place in a neighbourhood we'd never been to. So we
  figured we might as well create somewhere for people looking for new places in
  cool areas, centred around some of the best food in Toronto.
post: >-
  These places are awesome, and often run by such great people, often families
  that have built the business from scratch. I was in the west end at a place
  called Shadi Shawarma recently that we always used to go to after playing at
  Scarlett Woods in high school, a good 5–7 years ago. I walk in and the first
  thing the guy says is "I remember when you guys used to come in here after
  golf, how have you been".




  First, I look a lot different than I did then. Second, I'm from Cabbagetown and this place is at Bloor and Runnymede. It's not like I'm passing by all the time. I genuinely don't know how he remembered me, but he did and that was pretty cool.




  Every interaction matters to these folks. That's why these places are special and that's probably why we keep going back. Because you always feel welcomed there.
